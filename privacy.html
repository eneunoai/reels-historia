Reels Historia

Reels Historia is a personal tool that produces short videos about verified historical curiosities, narrated in Spanish, and publishes them to its owner's own TikTok account. It is used only by its owner. It does not offer accounts, sign-up or services to other people.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Data the app uses

  • From TikTok, after the owner authorises the app: the owner's basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) and the access tokens needed to publish videos.
  • The videos and captions the app creates.

How it is used

The data is used only to publish the owner's videos to the owner's own TikTok account and to check that each upload finished.

Storage and sharing

  • Tokens are stored only in a local file on the owner's computer. They are never uploaded to any server other than TikTok's own API.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.
  • The app does not collect any data about viewers or other TikTok users.

Deleting data

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok's settings under "Manage app permissions", and delete the local token file. Revoking access stops all publishing immediately.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent as a direct message to the TikTok account that publishes the videos.
